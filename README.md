# VU3INI-AudioUnmix-Studio-v1.0.5
Stand-alone Windows 10/11 application that splits any song into its separate parts, or “stems”. You get vocals, drums, bass, guitar, piano and other instruments, and you can preview, mix and export each one. Everything runs locally on your own PC. Your music is never uploaded anywhere, and after installation it works fully offline.

Main features:
AI separation with Meta’s Demucs and the community RoFormer/MDX models (4- and 6-stem modes).
Extra instruments: lead and backing vocals, male and female vocals, a full drum-kit split (kick, snare, toms, hi-hat, ride, crash), woodwinds, and crowd/applause removal.
Built-in mixer with volume, pan, mute/solo, looping, A/B against the original, and karaoke, a-cappella and drumless presets.
Export to WAV, FLAC, AIFF, MP3, OGG, Opus or M4A, or render a custom mix with high-quality tempo and pitch change.
MIDI export (new): turn stems into MIDI notes with Spotify’s Basic Pitch, and drums into General MIDI drum hits, ready for any DAW.
Reads almost any audio format (MP3, WAV, FLAC, OGG, AIFF, M4A and more), plus the sound from video files.
Runs on CPU, NVIDIA GPU, or both at once (RTX 50-series supported).
Easy to use: a Windows-style setup wizard, dark and light themes, full settings, built-in help, and an illustrated PDF guide.
Good for musicians practising along, karaoke, transcription, remixing, music teaching and study.
